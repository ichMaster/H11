## What changed beyond the fixes

- **Two new smoke assertions**, both negative-tested against the unfixed code: every
  pickup's texture matches its kind, and a corpse's modulate equals `Level.depth_shade()`.
- **A watchdog and structural bail-outs** in `--smoke`: a broken deck now exits 1 in
  seconds instead of hanging, which is how H11-002 was found — reproduced live while
  testing H11-001's fixtures.
- **`tools/check_audio.py`**, a new acceptance gate: decode, clipping, and a relative
  loop-seam threshold. It fails the old `ambient.wav` (seam 3380 vs a p99.9 internal
  step of 499) and passes the new one (131).
- **Release-safe deck validation** replaces a stripped `assert`, per
  [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §Failure modes.
