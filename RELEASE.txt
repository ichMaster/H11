Version 0.10.0 (2026-09-13)
---------------------------
- Background music: RustedMachinery.mp3 loops under the ambient bed. The hum is the
  room, the track is the score, and they play together rather than replace each other.
- M toggles music on and off, in both input profiles, with an on-screen confirmation.
  Not persisted: there is no settings store until v2.4.
- assets/music/ is the repository's first SUPPLIED asset directory, deliberately beside
  assets/sfx/ rather than inside it: everything in sfx/ is generated by
  tools/gen_sounds.py and overwritten wholesale on every run, so a hand-placed file
  there would survive exactly until the next regeneration.
- The weapon is a pump shotgun instead of a falling plasma note: dull and long. Energy
  below 200 Hz 72 -> 79%, mid scooped 24 -> 15%, 0.55 s against a 0.32 s fire cooldown
  so sustained fire overlaps the way a shotgun in a corridor does.
- The sound id is renamed laser -> shotgun.
- Build grows 64 -> 68 MB, the size of the track and nothing else.

NOTE ON ORDER: 0.9.0 is not cut yet. v0.9 (input as data) is four sixths done - its
seam shipped and this release depends on it, which is how a music toggle could be one
line in data/input.json - but two issues still need hands on the device (touch zones,
and confirming the 67-key layout). Released out of roadmap order at the owner's
request; 0.9.0 follows.

Version 0.8.0 (2026-09-12)
---------------------------
- First tagged release. Prototype 0 shipped unversioned; this is the hardening pass
  over it and the point from which the roadmap is released per phase.
- Doors show their whole texture: BoxMesh uses a 3x2 per-face UV atlas, so every door
  had been showing a magnified sixth - never the RED CLEARANCE block that marks one
  locked.
- Killed mutants are depth-shaded instead of staying flash-red forever.
- Ammo cells and keycards wear their own sprites; they had all rendered as medkits.
- Floor and ceiling fog is evaluated per fragment, not at the four corners of the deck.
- ambient.wav loops without a click: seam 3380 -> 131 against a typical step of ~495.
- Decks are validated at load with a release-safe check, not a stripped assert.
- --smoke exits 1 on a structural failure instead of hanging, and gained two
  assertions (18 checks, was 16).
- New acceptance gate tools/check_audio.py: decode, clipping, loop-seam continuity.
- Deploy copies before it kills and swaps atomically; a failed copy leaves the device
  running its previous build.
- The desktop scale survives a crash: persisted to a state file, self-healed on the
  next launch.
- The asset generator refuses to write art that failed its own alpha check, and fails
  loudly on an unknown --only name.
- Mockups render at the fog the game actually uses.

Measured on the device: 2.97 ms mean frame, 3.33 ms worst, against a 16.67 ms budget.
